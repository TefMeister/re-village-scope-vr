# A faint glass shine over the scope picture, all the time

Order: 4005
From: mod-ideas `games/re-village.md` (<https://github.com/TefMeister/mod-ideas/blob/main/games/re-village.md>), copied 2026-09-23

`[raw]` · `[looks doable]` — *not tried; a visuals idea, filed here beside its sibling above*

> "wild idea, can there be a layer of reflection applied to our scope picture, so it looks like
> there is a shine to it, like glass does? probably a stupid idea." — 2026-09-21, right after the
> near/far shots landed

Not a stupid idea. At the moment the scope shows a perfectly clean picture, which is exactly what
gives it away as a screen stuck on the end of a tube. Real glass always carries a little of the
world on its surface — a soft highlight that slides across as the rifle moves. Adding that is what
would make it read as a **lens** rather than a **monitor**.

It is the everyday version of the idea above: that one brings the glass back *only* when the
picture fails; this one keeps a faint trace of it *always*, and the two would blend naturally — the
shine simply grows as the eye leaves the sweet spot.

**What it'd take:** the mod already draws the scope picture onto the glass itself, in its own step,
so laying a faint highlight over the top in that same step is a small addition rather than a new
system. Two ways to get the highlight: a simple painted streak of light that shifts with the
rifle's tilt (cheap, fully ours), or borrowing the game's own lens material, which already has the
shine built in (better looking, but see the warning above — not known whether it can be shown on
demand). It must stay faint and must never sit over the crosshair centre, or it costs accuracy for
looks.

⚠️ **Unchecked.** Nothing here has been tried against the game. In particular, whether a highlight
looks right in **both eyes** in the headset is a wear-it-and-see question — a shine that sits at
the wrong depth would look like dirt on the lens, not a reflection.
