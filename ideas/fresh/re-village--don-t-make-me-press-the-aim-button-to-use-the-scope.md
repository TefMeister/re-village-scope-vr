# Don't make me press the aim button to use the scope

Order: 4002
From: mod-ideas `games/re-village.md` (<https://github.com/TefMeister/mod-ideas/blob/main/games/re-village.md>), copied 2026-09-23

`[raw]` · `[not judged]` — *deliberately unjudged; see the warning at the end*

> "i really do want to not have to press RG for aiming, it is one step that makes the hands
> dissapear and moves the player view just slightly, but in a very annoying way, like in a way that
> makes me not want to aim the gun in VR, hard to wxplain." — 2026-09-20

In VR you already aim by physically lifting the rifle to your eye. Being made to press a button as
well to tell the game you are aiming is a second, invented step — and it is not free: the hands
vanish and the view shifts slightly. Small on paper; **the actual reported effect is not wanting to
aim the gun at all**, which for a sniper rifle mod is as bad as a fault gets.

⭐ **Worth noticing that this is a VR-native complaint.** A flat player presses aim because there is
no other way to say it. In a headset the gesture IS the statement, so the button becomes a thing
that undoes what your arms just said.

⚠️ **NOT JUDGED, ON PURPOSE, and this is the important part.** The aim state is believed to be what
NARROWS THE BULLET SPREAD (dossier, `[hypothesis]`). So "just don't require the button" may mean
"shoot with hip-fire accuracy forever", which is the opposite of what is wanted — the same person
also asked for spread to be pinned near zero the same evening. **The two are one problem**: what is
really wanted is the *accuracy* of aiming without the *ceremony* of it.

Guessing a buildability tag before the spread mechanism is understood would repeat exactly the
mistake the spread idea itself made (it was marked `[looks doable]` on the assumption spread was
"almost certainly just a number"; it is not). So this one waits for the dig.
