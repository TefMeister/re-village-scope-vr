===========================================================================
 SCOPE AIM TEST  --  step by step            written 2026-09-20
===========================================================================

Nothing to install. The right build is already in place and last night's dead
code is switched off. You only need the game and the rifle.

The three test scripts are LIVE - they take effect in a second or two, with no
relaunch and no rebuilding. So once the scope is up you can flip between them
as often as you like.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 1  --  GET THE SCOPE WORKING        (needed once per launch)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Launch the game and load your save.

 2. Get the SNIPER RIFLE in your hands. Nothing below works without it.

 3. Double-click   START-SCOPE.bat

 4. A black window opens and counts through four steps.
    *** LEAVE IT ALONE UNTIL IT CLOSES ITSELF (about 50 seconds). ***
    The picture usually appears after a second or two - that is normal, and
    you can look at it - but the LAST step is what aims the picture at your
    eye. Close the window early and you will see Ethan's clothes and the
    ground instead of the view.

 5. Look through the scope. You should have a picture.

    If instead you see:
      - brown and gold streaks (the inside of the scope tube) -> tell me
      - black                                                 -> tell me
      - Ethan's clothes / the ground                          -> the window
        was closed early; just run START-SCOPE.bat again and let it finish.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 2  --  THE BASELINE           (this is what everything is compared to)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

 6. Double-click   AIM-TEST-B-DETAILED.bat        (this is "normal")

 7. Stand still. Look straight ahead. Hold the rifle level and scoped.

 8. Keeping your head still and looking straight ahead, SLOWLY RAISE THE
    RIFLE until the picture streaks and smears.

 9. Remember roughly how far up you got before it went. You do not need a
    number - "barely moved", "about halfway up", "almost straight up" is
    exactly the right kind of answer.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 3  --  TEST C          (the promising one - this is the real question)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

10. Double-click   AIM-TEST-C-NO-EYE-TILT.bat

11. Do exactly the same as step 8: level, then slowly raise until it streaks.

12. THE QUESTION:  MORE room than normal, LESS room, or THE SAME?

    Ignore where it points and how it looks. Only the ROOM matters here -
    how far you can raise the rifle before it streaks.

    What I expect if my guess is right: noticeably more room, and the
    zeroing (where the shot lands) unchanged.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 4  --  TEST A                            (a different question: AIM)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

13. Double-click   AIM-TEST-A-SIMPLE.bat

14. Do NOT raise the rifle this time. Just point at something ordinary and
    look through the scope.

15. THE QUESTION:  does it show what you are pointing at BETTER or WORSE
    than normal?

    The picture may look stretched or skewed under this one. That is
    expected and is not the point - judge only WHERE IT POINTS.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PART 5  --  PUT IT BACK
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

16. Double-click   AIM-TEST-B-DETAILED.bat

    That restores both settings. (Until 2026-09-20 it only restored one of
    them, which would have made the baseline secretly identical to test C.)


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT TO TELL ME
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    C compared with B:   more room / less room / the same
    A compared with B:   aims better / aims worse / the same

 Anything else you notice is worth saying too - especially things blinking
 in and out at the edges of the scope picture while you move.

 If you close the game at any point, start again from PART 1.
